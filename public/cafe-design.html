小镇一角 · 咖啡馆 A Corner of Town — Café design sheet

Design only, nothing built yet. Every object has one fixed place. The player never picks anything; they find where a sticker goes and press it down. Drawings are to scale: in the elevations and floor plan, 1 grid square = 10 cm. Solid outline = model already exists, dashed = new.

1 · 墙的尺寸 Wall size

Recommendation: make each wall 320 cm wide with a 250 cm ceiling (today it is 400 cm wide and about 316 cm high).

  • Legibility. The half-canvas is 1000 px wide, so 400 cm gives 2.5 px/cm and 320 cm gives 3.125 px/cm. Everything gets 25% bigger. A 6 cm sugar bowl goes from 15 px to 19 px, and a 30 cm machine from 75 px to 94 px.
  • Ceiling. With FLOOR_LINE at canvas y 790, 320 cm leaves 790 / 3.125 ≈ 253 cm above the floor. That is a real café ceiling (250 cm) with no empty band at the top. 400 cm shows 316 cm, and the top 70 cm of that is dead wall.
  • Room for items. 320 cm still takes a door plus a 180 cm counter on the left wall, and a reading nook plus a 160 cm brewing bench on the right. About 50 stickers per wall fit because they stack vertically (counter → back-bar shelves → lamps) and the small things live in zoom regions. A narrower wall would have to drop the door or the nook.
  • Small items are solved by zoom, not by wall size. Shrinking the wall to 250 cm would still leave beans at ~5 px. Zoom regions of about 90–130 cm × 65–80 cm are shown at ~2.5–3.5×, so each cm becomes 8–11 px.
  • Cost. One constant (half width 400 → 320) in clay/README.md/pipeline, plus moving the objects marked “move” below. The room becomes 320 × 320 cm.

2 · 平面图 Floor plan (top-down)

The corner is at the top right. The left wall (shop front) runs along the top. Its u = 0 end, far from the corner, has the glass door. The right wall (seating) runs down the right side, and its u = 0 is the corner. The walkway runs from the door, diagonally across the rug, to the reading nook. The café table sits on the rug so the player can “see” where a customer would sit.

7 · 待定问题 Open questions (things I guessed)

  1. Which end is the corner? I assumed left-wall u = 320 and right-wall u = 0 meet at the corner, matching world x ∈ [−400, 0] → corner at x = 0. If the pipeline maps it the other way, mirror the left wall (door at the corner end).
  2. Door: architecture or sticker? I drew the door and the windows as fixed background (not carry-able). The “OPEN” sign hanging on the door is a sticker.
  3. Moving the bookshelf. The 10 books + shelf currently sit above the espresso machine. I moved them to the right wall above the armchair (reading nook) and gave the bar real back-bar shelves for jars and cups instead. OK?
  4. Pendant lamps hang from the ceiling, which has no plane. I treated them as mount: "wall" stickers whose cord reaches the top edge.
  5. Zoom factor. I assumed the zoom view fills the same 1000 px canvas width. Regions are ~90–130 cm wide → 2.5–3.5×. If the zoom view is smaller (e.g. a phone), regions should be narrower.
  6. Sideboard width. 320 cm only fits the brewing bench if sideboard uses its width option at 160 (from 180), with the six brewing tools at ~25 cm spacing.
  7. Café table + stools stand ~110 cm out from the wall, so in 2D they overlap the bench's lower half. Is that depth-overlap fine, or should the table go in front of the window instead?
  8. Cats are left as an optional slot (window sill on the right wall, 30 × 20 cm, plus the rug). Nothing depends on them, and they are not counted.
  9. Rug is flat; on the right-wall 2D view it shows as a thin strip at the floor line. Should it belong to one wall, or appear only after the unfold?
