# Crit 9 reflection

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

There were two breakthroughs. The first was moving to 3D. At crit 8 the
scene was made of flat stickers, and you could only look at it from one side.
Now it is a small clay world, and that is simply more interesting: you can
circle around your town and your shops and look at them from many angles.

The second was that I learned to manage the work by dividing it between
agents. Before, everything went through one conversation in a straight line,
one change after another, and it got messy. Now each agent has its own job —
one for the cameras, one for the clay models, one for the interface, one for
phones, and one that only checks. Because their jobs do not overlap, they can
work in parallel, and the whole thing moves much faster.

**What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?**

I want to be a developer who uses AI sensibly. This project made me realise
how powerful AI is — its efficiency is extremely high. So how to direct it
and keep it under control is a skill of its own. Language is too vague; it
cannot express exactly what I mean. Dividing the work clearly is one way to
deal with that, because each agent only needs to understand its own part.
Learning how to use AI well is something that really matters.
