# Crit 9 reflection

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

The breakthrough was moving to 3D. The scene is no longer flat stickers; it
is a small clay world you can step into.

Two other things mattered. One I already had at crit 8, so I will only
mention it: a small editor where I fix a position myself and then hand the
result to the AI still works better than telling it directly what I want. The
other is new: dividing the work between agents, each with its own job. That
turned out to be important too.

**What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?**

I want to be a developer who uses AI sensibly. This project made me realise
how powerful AI is — its efficiency is extremely high. So how to direct it
and keep it under control is a skill of its own. Language is too vague; it
cannot express exactly what I mean. Learning how to use AI well is something
that really matters.
